Delete your Otuuse Transport account

You can ask us to delete your Otuuse Transport account and the personal data linked to it at any time. This page explains how.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to info@beta-techlabs.com with the subject Delete my Otuuse account.
  2. In the email, include the full name and the phone number registered on your account, and say whether you are a passenger or a driver.
  3. We may reply to confirm the request is really from you before we delete anything.
  4. We complete the deletion within 30 days of confirming your request, and tell you when it is done.
Drivers: if your wallet still has a balance, withdraw it in the app before you ask for deletion, or tell us in your email and we will arrange the payout first.

What we delete

  • Your name, phone number and password.
  • Your chat messages and delivery requests.
  • Photos you uploaded for deliveries.
  • Location data and ratings linked to your account.

What we may keep

We keep records that we are required to keep for legal, tax or accounting reasons, such as wallet transactions and completed ride payments. Where we keep them, they are separated from your identity as far as we can.

Questions

Contact us at info@beta-techlabs.com. You can also read our Privacy Policy.
