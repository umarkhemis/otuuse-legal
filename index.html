Otuuse Transport - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Otuuse Transport ("Otuuse", "we", "us") is a boda boda ride-booking and local delivery service for Kabale, Uganda, operated by Beta-Tech Labs. This policy explains what information the Otuuse Transport mobile app collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to passengers and drivers who use the app.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, phone number and password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash, never in readable form.
  • Location: drivers share their precise GPS location while they are online, so we can match them with nearby passengers and calculate fares. Passengers give pickup and destination places by typing them in chat; we convert those places to map coordinates. We do not track location when the app is closed, and the app does not collect background location.
  • Ride and delivery information: pickup and destination, fare, distance, route, ride status, payment method (cash or mobile money), and the item, pickup and drop-off details of delivery requests.
  • Chat messages: the messages you send to the Otuuse assistant, and the replies you receive from the assistant and from our admin team.
  • Photos: pictures you choose to attach to a delivery request, and pictures our admin team attaches in reply. We only access your camera or gallery when you choose to attach a photo.
  • Payment records: driver wallet top-ups, withdrawals and commission deductions. We do not see or store your mobile money PIN or card details.
  • Ratings and reviews that passengers and drivers give each other after a ride.
  • Device and notification data: a push notification token for your device, and basic technical logs (such as request times and errors) used to keep the service running.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To match passengers with nearby drivers, calculate routes and fares, and complete rides.
  • To handle delivery requests through our admin team.
  • To understand your chat messages and respond to them.
  • To process driver wallet payments and deduct the platform commission.
  • To send ride and delivery notifications.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, resolve disputes, and improve the service.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertising in the app.

3. Who we share information with

Between users. When a driver accepts your ride, the driver sees your name, phone number, pickup and destination. You see the driver's name, phone number and motorcycle plate number. After a ride, ratings you give are visible to our team and affect the other person's rating.

Our admin team. Authorised Otuuse administrators can see delivery requests, the name and phone number of the person who made them, and the related conversation so they can respond and arrange the delivery.

Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law, a court order, or to protect the safety of users and the public.

4. Payments

Passengers pay drivers directly, in cash or by mobile money. Otuuse does not hold passenger funds. Drivers keep a prepaid wallet with us; the platform commission for each ride is deducted from that wallet. Wallet top-ups and withdrawals are processed by PesaPal. We keep a record of these transactions.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your account information and history for as long as your account is active. Transaction records may be kept longer where we need them for accounting, legal or dispute purposes. Clearing the chat inside the app clears it from your screen; to have stored messages deleted, send us a deletion request as described in section 7.

6. Security

Data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed, access to admin tools requires an admin login, and we limit who can see personal data. No system is completely secure, so please choose a strong password and keep it private.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or delete your account entirely. To make a request, email us at info@beta-techlabs.com from the phone number or email you registered with, or contact our admin team through the app. We will respond within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days. When an account is deleted we remove or anonymise personal data, except records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons.

You can also turn off location and camera permissions at any time in your phone settings, although drivers cannot receive ride requests without location access.

This policy is intended to meet the requirements of Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

8. Children

Otuuse Transport is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last revised. If we make significant changes we will tell you in the app.
